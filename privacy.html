In short: News_Hub has no accounts and doesn't ask you for personal information to browse. We only receive your details if you contact us. Some content on the site is loaded from third-party services, which can see basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests it.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. If you use the contact form, it opens your email app with your name, email address and message filled in. We receive that information only if you send the email, and we use it to reply to you.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic data when you visit, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. Third-party services

To work, News_Hub relies on other services. When your browser loads content from them, they receive your IP address and device details and handle that data under their own policies.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on Vercel.
  • News data: headlines, summaries and images are fetched from third-party news APIs, including the Spaceflight News API on the home page and the news sources used by the Search and category pages.
  • Images: some page images are loaded from Unsplash.

We don't control these services. Please review their privacy policies if you want to know how they handle data.

3. Cookies and storage

News_Hub does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services listed above may use their own technical storage. You can block or clear cookies and site data in your browser settings; the site should still work.

4. Links to other sites

Story links take you to publishers' websites. Their content and privacy practices are outside our control, so we encourage you to read their policies before sharing information with them.

5. Children

News_Hub is a general news site and is not directed at children under 15. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold from your messages, ask us to correct or delete it, or stop receiving replies from us at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection laws. Contact us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email on "contact.newshub.org@gmail.com" or use the contact page.
