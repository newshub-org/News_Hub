How it works

From question to story in three steps.

  1. Pick a topic

    Choose a category such as World, Technology, Sports or Business, or type your own keyword.

  2. Scan the headlines

    See titles, sources and dates at a glance so you can decide what is worth your time.

  3. Read the original

    Open the story on the publisher's website to get the full article.

Transparency

Where stories come from.

You should always know who wrote what you are reading.

Credit to publishers

News_Hub links to stories from their original publishers. Headlines, summaries and images belong to those publishers, and each story shows its source and date.

Our principles

  • We do not edit or rewrite publishers' articles.
  • We always show the source.
  • We keep the site free of clutter.
FAQ

Common questions.

Is News_Hub free to use?

Yes. You can search and browse every category without an account.

Does News_Hub write the news?

No. We help you discover stories and send you to the original publisher for the full article.

How can I report a problem or suggest a topic?

Use the contact page and tell us what you found or what you would like to see.
